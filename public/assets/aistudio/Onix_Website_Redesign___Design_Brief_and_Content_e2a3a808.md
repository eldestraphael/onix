# **Onix Website Redesign — Design Brief and Content** 

Oct 19, 2026 · @Samuel 

## **The brief** 

Redesign onixdatacentres.com so that a cautious buyer leaves certain that choosing Onix is the safe decision, and books a tour. All copy for the new site is in this document, page by page. Square brackets mark facts we'll supply before launch. 

**About Onix:** Onix Data Centres runs West Africa's first Uptime Institute–certified Tier IV data centre, in Accra, Ghana, operating since 2019. We also offer colocation at the 2Africa cable landing station in Dakar, Senegal. Onix is Ghanaian and African owned and operated. 

**The problem with today's site:** it describes a building in generic language ("state-of-the-art", "cuttingedge"). Our strongest proof is buried, our people are invisible, and some content is out of date. Buyers can't tell us apart from the global operators now in Accra. 

#### **Goals, in order** 

1. More qualified tour bookings: the tour is our main conversion. 

2. Make our proof impossible to miss: certification, track record, power resilience and people. 

3. Help a buyer's internal champion win the argument, through the downloadable board pack. 

4. Make Onix findable and credible to international buyers. 

#### **Who we're designing for** 

|Audience|What they fear|What the site must show them|
|---|---|---|
|Bank and fintech CIOs,<br>CISOs, risk teams|An outage or failed audit that<br>lands on them|Certification, dated certificates, named<br>engineers, a visit|
|Telcos, ISPs, content<br>networks|Thin interconnection, slow<br>cross-connects|Carriers, Meet-Me Room detail, lead<br>times|
|Enterprise and public<br>sector|Their in-house server room<br>failing in a power cut|What it costs to do it themselves, and<br>how moving works|
|International carriers<br>and buyers|An unknown partner in an<br>unfamiliar market|Independent certification, operating data,<br>named contacts, a video tour|



**The one idea: "Come and check."** Where the category shouts, we show. Every page should prove rather than claim, and every page ends with an invitation to inspect. 

**Primary action on every page:** Book a tour. **Secondary actions:** Talk to an engineer; Get the board pack. **Design for:** a busy CIO on a phone between meetings, often on a variable connection. Fast, clear, and scannable in 10 seconds. 

## **Brand, tone and visual direction** 

Keep the brand codes people already know, and change how the content is presented. A redesign is the moment to make Onix _more_ recognisable, not less. 

#### **Keep** 

- The ONIX red wordmark, the navy and the white. Use red sparingly, for the primary button and key highlights. 

- The tagline "Connecting Africa to the Globe", in the footer only. 

#### **Tone: three words, each with its opposite** 

- Confident, not loud. 

- Precise, not technical for its own sake. 

- Warm, not salesy. 

**Words we don't use:** state-of-the-art, world-class, cutting-edge, unparalleled, robust, seamless, leading provider, best-in-class. Replace each with a number, a name or a date. 

#### **Photography** 

|Use|Avoid|
|---|---|
|Our real facility: the floor, the generators, the solar<br>farm in daylight, the Meet-Me Room|Stock server corridors with blue glow, globes,<br>binary code, handshakes|
|Our real people, named, at work and looking at the<br>camera|Models, anonymous hands on keyboards|
|Visitors on a tour, with permission|Empty rooms|
|Accra context: the site, the raised building, the road<br>in|Generic "Africa" imagery|



A one-day professional shoot at the facility is the single most valuable design investment. Brief the photographer with the shot list in the section on facts to confirm. 

#### **How proof should look** 

- Numbers are big, with a short source line under each ("Certified by Uptime Institute", "Measured [period]"). 

- Certificates appear as real documents: thumbnail, issuer, number, expiry date, download link. 

- People appear with name, role and one line in their own words. 

- One honest limitation is said openly (the 25 km block on the homepage). Design it as a confident statement, not a footnote. 

#### **Layout principles** 

- One message per screen. Short paragraphs, a maximum of three sentences. 

- "Book a tour" is always visible: in the header on desktop, and in a sticky bottom bar on mobile. 

- WhatsApp is a normal business channel in Ghana. Make it visible, but secondary to the tour button.

## **Technical, accessibility and SEO requirements** 

#### **Performance** 

- Mobile first. Test on a mid-range Android phone over a slow 3G/4G connection. 

- Target under [2.5 s] Largest Contentful Paint on mobile. Compress and lazy-load images, and serve modern formats (WebP/AVIF). 

- Key numbers and text must be in the HTML, not only revealed by animation or JavaScript. 

#### **Accessibility** 

- WCAG 2.1 AA: colour contrast (check white on red and the navy backgrounds), visible keyboard focus, alt text on every image, labelled form fields. 

- Tap targets at least 44 × 44 px. The sticky mobile tour bar must not cover content or forms.
